Combined — Broker to Read Refresh (Consume Side)

Role: End to end on the consume side: an approved-purchase-order event travels the broker, passes inbox dedupe, invalidates GRN caches, and the next read rebuilds.

Contents · Flow · Sequence

Flow

steps in order
1OutboxScheduler claims the PurchaseOrderApproved row and publishes it with broker rabbitmq while the bus re-emits in process.
2RabbitMQ delivers to the grn-cache-invalidation queue; the listener claims it through InboxService.
3Duplicates return DUPLICATE or BUSY and skip; ACQUIRED deletes the purchase-order and GRN cache keys.
4Inbox complete marks PROCESSED with CAS (Compare-And-Swap) before broker ACK.
5Next goods-receipt read misses, falls through to the replica, stores back with TTL, and serves fresh data.
