Goods Receipt Note — Receive plus Complete

Role: Guarded receive: idempotency plus per-document lock, line accumulation with receivedAt stamp, cache invalidation, guarded release.

Contents · Flow · Sequence

Flow

steps in order
1Client posts receive, add-line, complete, or cancel; controller reserves idempotency with Redis check plus Postgres claim.
2Guard phase: acquire the distributed lock for the GRN id (SET NX PX, owner token; serializes document receives).
3Execute phase: the aggregate loads with its lines, addLine accumulates ordered versus received quantities, receive stamps receivedAt and needs at least one line, complete or cancel close it; invariants enforced, events raised.
4Command repository saves through the mapper on TransactionHost into Postgres; pullEvents drains; OutboxAdapter appends in the same transaction; read cache deleted via Redis DEL.
5Idempotency complete stores COMPLETED with CAS (Compare-And-Swap) plus resultJson and writes Redis; lock release runs Lua DEL in finally.
6Async: scheduler claims the batch with the lease clause, publishes per broker, re-emits in process, and settles.
